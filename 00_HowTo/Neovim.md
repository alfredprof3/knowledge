#type/HowTo #topic/Neovim/Commands #for/Debian 

- To enter in Terminal-mode inside a buffer, type:
  `:terminal`
- To leave Terminal-mode, type:
  `<C-\><C-N>`
- To leave Terminal-mode in macOS with a macOS keyboard layout, type:
  `⌃ ⌥ ?` + `⌃ N`
- To leave Terminal-mode in PC with a macOS keyboard layout, type:
  `⌃ ⌘ ?` + `⌃ N`
- To leave Terminal-mode in PC, type:
  `Ctrl AltGr ?` + `Ctrl N`
