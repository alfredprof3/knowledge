# Remove people from your business portfolio in Meta Business Suite
Source: [Remove people from your business portfolio in Meta Business Suite](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/353397428740939/)

You can remove someone from a [business portfolio](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/486932075688253), provided you have [full control](https://facebook.com/business/help/442345745885606) of the business portfolio.

Removing a person revokes their access to the business portfolio and any [business assets](https://business.facebook.com/business/help/1242256693745272)(examples: Facebook Page, Instagram account, ad account) they have access to.

**Note:** Removing people from a business portfolio is different to [removing partners](https://facebook.com/business/help/2222659621282858).

## Before you begin

- You have [full control](https://facebook.com/business/help/442345745885606) of the business portfolio.
    
    - Learn how to [submit a request to get full control of a business portfolio](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/474856681929983) if no one in your organization currently has full control of the business portfolio.
    
- The person you’re removing is [in your organization’s business portfolio](https://facebook.com/business/help/708679622611131). If you want to remove someone from a business asset that isn’t part of your organization’s business portfolio, learn how to:
    
    - [Remove someone from a Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/help/187316341316631)
        
    - [Manage shared access on Instagram](https://l.facebook.com/l.php?u=https%3A%2F%2Fhelp.instagram.com%2F514043488379093&h=AUAMRY0rN_Ikms5_1yCtMGKxxtYx65u3GJHEdCgNYomxjgyxh2MK0BWDgZooJWI0bnRdUGXohRyVgl8WDq7GHqtR4XHvASSM9BDzfkFgLj8hS0h7YJU1iajA_eJGyOt9bqf4AemQZU_t-v_X4slBDW5ZKUtOI3C6QvNXUqj9BHQ)
        
    - [Remove someone from your ad account in Meta Ads Manager](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/186007118118684)
    
- Learn other [best practices](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/608572510632070) for keeping your business portfolio secure.

## Remove someone from your business portfolio

To remove someone from your business portfolio in Meta Business Suite:

1. Go to [**Settings**](https://business.facebook.com/latest/settings) in Meta Business Suite for your business portfolio.
2. Click **People** in the left menu.
    - If you cannot see **People**, then you may not have full control of the business portfolio.
        
3. Click the name of the person you want to remove.
4. Click **Remove**, then confirm you want to remove the person from the business portfolio.

**Note:** If someone else with full control of the business portfolio needs to approve the removal of this person, then they can do this in [**Requests**](https://business.facebook.com/latest/settings/requests) in Meta Business Suite’s Settings.
