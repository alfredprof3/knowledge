# Remove yourself from a business portfolio
Source: [Remove yourself from a business portfolio](https://business.facebook.com/business/help/302157920389896/)

You can remove yourself from a business portfolio. Once you're removed, you'll no longer have access to the business portfolio, including any of its Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts or other business assets.

## Remove yourself from a Business Account

To remove yourself from a business portfolio in:

**Meta Business Suite**

1. Go to [**Settings**](https://business.facebook.com/latest/settings).
2. From the **People** tab, select your name.
3. Click ![20](https://static.xx.fbcdn.net/assets/?set=content_operations_help_center&name=fb-3dotssquarebutton-desktop&density=1)**Options** and select **Remove from business portfolio**.
4. Click **Remove**.
