> The most common payment gateway options used by online stores today vary based on their technical needs, target markets, and platform choice. ==Stripe, PayPal, and Adyen== are consistently identified as the leading global choices for 2026.
> 
> Below are the most frequently used payment gateways, categorized by their primary strengths:
> 
> ## Top Global Payment Gateways
> 
> - Stripe: A dominant "developer-first" platform used by millions of companies globally. It is favored for its robust API, clean checkout experience, and support for over 135 currencies.
> - PayPal: Widely considered the ultimate trust-builder, appearing on roughly 20 million websites by 2026. It acts as both a gateway and a payment method, boasting a 45.52% market share in the online processing environment.
> - Adyen: An enterprise-grade powerhouse that provides a unified global infrastructure for online, mobile, and in-store channels. It is used by major brands like Spotify and Uber.
> - Authorize.Net: One of the oldest and most recognized gateways, especially popular in the US, UK, Canada, and Europe. It is frequently chosen for its speed and fraud prevention tools.
> 
> ## Platform-Specific & Omnichannel Gateways
> 
> - Shopify Payments: Built directly into the Shopify ecosystem, it simplifies setup by eliminating third-party fees and unlocking features like Shop Pay.
> - Square: Primarily used by retailers who blend physical and digital sales. It is popular for its seamless point-of-sale integration.
> - Wix Payments: An internal solution for Wix users that allows customers to pay without being redirected to a third-party site.
> - Braintree: Owned by PayPal, it is used by approximately 34,000 to 44,000 websites and is popular in industries like travel, retail, and SaaS.
> 
> ## Region-Specific & Specialized Gateways
> 
> - Mercado Pago: The leading option in Mexico and Latin America, deeply integrated with the Mercado Libre ecosystem.
> - PayU: A prominent global provider with a strong presence in Mexico and high-growth markets.
> - Conekta & Openpay: These are top-tier specialized gateways for the Mexican market, offering optimized processing for local payment methods like SPEI and cash vouchers.
> - Airwallex: Recognized as a top pick for global commerce, allowing high-growth e-commerce brands to settle in 20+ currencies without traditional banking friction.
> - Klarna & Afterpay: Leading "Buy Now, Pay Later" (BNPL) gateways that allow customers to pay in installments.
> - BitPay: A recognized name for crypto payments, offering tools for large enterprises to accept digital currencies and get settled in fiat.
> 
> ## Summary of Most Common Digital Wallets (Payment Methods)
> 
> While often used via the gateways above, these digital wallets are essential checkout components:
> 
> - Apple Pay: Accounts for nearly half (45.5%) of all in-store digital wallet purchases and is increasingly standard for online shopping.
> - Google Pay: Reported to reduce cart abandonment rates by up to 55% for top global retailers.
> - Amazon Pay: Leverages existing Amazon credentials to provide a one-click checkout experience.
