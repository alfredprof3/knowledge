List of real-time AI Translator apps.
1. [JotMe](https://www.jotme.io): Specifically designed for remote teams, it offers real-time translation and transcription across Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams. It also generates AI-powered meeting summaries.
2. [Wordly](https://www.wordly.ai): Ideal for large-scale events and webinars. It provides real-time captioning in multiple languages simultaneously, allowing attendees to follow along on their own devices.
3. [KUDO AI](https://kudo.ai): An enterprise-grade platform that combines AI translation with the option to book professional human interpreters for high-stakes meetings.
4. [Talo](https://taloai.com): Uses a single AI bot to translate all participants in a meeting, supporting over 60 languages with high security standards like SOC 2.
5. [Google Translate](https://translate.google.com/?sl=es&tl=el&op=translate): Features a dedicated Conversation Mode that splits the screen between two languages, making it easy for two people to talk back-and-forth. It is completely free and supports offline translation for many languages.
6. [iTranslate](https://itranslate.com): Offers a highly-rated mobile experience with voice-to-voice translation and a camera feature for translating signs and menus.
7. SayHi Translate: A simple, focused app for natural one-on-one conversations. It is often cited as one of the best for casual, instantaneous speech translation.
8. Apple Translate: For iPhone users, this is a privacy-focused, built-in option that handles 18 major languages with high accuracy and on-device processing.
9. [AI Phone](https://www.aiphone.ai): Designed specifically for phone call translation. It translates both ends of a call in real-time, providing both audio and text captions.
10. [Maestra AI](https://maestra.ai): Best for creators and educators, offering real-time dubbing and subtitle generation for videos and live streams across 125+ languages.
11. [Timekettle](https://www.timekettle.co): While also an app, it is built to work with dedicated hardware like translator earbuds, providing a near-seamless "natural" conversation experience.

> **Key Features to Look For**
> 
> 1. **Latency**: For a natural conversation, look for apps with "near-zero" or low latency (like **PolyPal** or **[Transync AI](https://www.transyncai.com/)**) to avoid awkward pauses.
> 2. **Offline Support**: Crucial for travel where data might be spotty. **Google Translate** and **iTranslate** (Pro) are leaders here.
> 3. **Accuracy & Context**: AI-driven apps like **[DeepL](https://www.deepl.com/en)** and **QuillBot** prioritize context over literal word-for-word translation, which is vital for business.
