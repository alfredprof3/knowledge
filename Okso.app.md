#Whiteboard #Online #Tool #alternative #TLDRAW #Excalidraw 

Okso.app is a whiteboard online tool that stores files locally in the cache of your computer. If you want to synchronize your files you need to download the file and open in another computer using your favorite browser. ==_Live Collaboration_== is coming soon.
